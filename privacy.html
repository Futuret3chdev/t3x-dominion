Privacy

T3X Dominion is a game by Futuret3Ch. This page covers the browser game and the Facebook Instant Game.

What the game stores

Progress, cards, and settings are saved in your browser on this device. The game does not ask you to create an account. Clearing the site data on your device removes that save.

Facebook

The Instant Game uses Meta’s Zero Permissions connection. It does not ask for your email, friends list, or permission to post. Facebook still runs the session under its own policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell player data. The game does not take card payments inside the browser build.
