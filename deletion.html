Delete your data

T3X Dominion does not create a Futuret3Ch account. Progress, cards, and settings are stored in the browser on the device you played on.

On your phone or computer

  1. Open the browser you used to play.
  2. Clear website data for t3x-dominion.vercel.app and t3x.futuret3ch.com.au.
  3. On iPhone, that is Settings, then Safari or your browser, then clear history and website data. On Chrome, open the site, then the lock icon, then site settings, then delete data.

That removes the save. We do not keep a copy on a server.

If you played on Facebook

Remove T3X Dominion from your Facebook games or apps. The Instant Game does not ask for your email, friends list, or permission to post. Facebook’s own data is handled by Meta.

Questions: use the contact email on the Futuret3Ch Facebook app, or message Futuret3Ch on Facebook.
