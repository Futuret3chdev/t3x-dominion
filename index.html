    How this war works

    The goal

    Own 31 hexes — half of 61. You also win if the enemy has no units and no land. You lose if they hit 31 first, or you lose both your army and your land.

    Colors

    Teal is yours, bottom of the map. Crimson is the enemy, at the top. Grey is nobody’s. A gold diamond is a relay.

    Your turn

    Bright hexes are legal moves. Red hexes are legal attacks. Each unit may move, attack, or repair once. New units can act the same turn. You cannot walk through a unit.

    Capturing

    Stepping onto grey or crimson ground makes it yours. Destroying a unit does not take the hex — step onto the empty hex afterwards.

    Energy and Data

    Energy pays for units. Data only pays for repairs. Repair restores up to 40 health, costs 10 Data, and uses that unit’s action. End of turn pay: 8 Energy + 4 per hex + 12 per relay, and 3 Data + 2 per relay.

    Units — both sides use these

    Scout, 20 Energy. Health 40. Damage 12. Walks 2. Fragile land-grabber.
    Defender, 35. Health 110. Damage 10. Walks 1. A wall.
    Attacker, 50. Health 75. Damage 28. Must stand next to them.
    Artillery, 65. Health 50. Damage 34. Shoots 2 hexes away, through other units.

    Enemy turn

    After you end the turn, their units move one at a time and the log says what they did.
